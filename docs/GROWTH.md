# The Guild — Growth Constitution

**v1.0 — Constitution for how Guild grows for the next decade.**

Read this before coach outreach, parent campaigns, partnerships, or any decision that affects supply, demand, or geographic expansion. Plans expire. **This document does not.**

Future changes to this document should be **deliberate and infrequent**. Campaigns and tactics evolve around it.

Companion docs:

- `docs/VISION.md` — what Guild is becoming (product north star)
- `docs/GROWTH.md` — how Guild gets there (this file)
- `docs/OPERATIONS.md` — *future* — how we run day to day
- `docs/CULTURE.md` — *future* — coach quality, responsiveness, professionalism, premium brand, athlete first, no shortcuts

One without the other leaves a gap between strategy and execution.

## Guild Growth Philosophy

Growth is not measured by traffic.

Growth is not measured by applications.

Growth is not measured by signups.

**Guild grows when more coaches consistently earn money through the platform.**

Every growth decision should strengthen the product flywheel — not bypass it.

See `docs/VISION.md` for product pillars, current state, and anti-goals.

---

## The product flywheel (growth must accelerate it)

```
Great coaches
      ↓
More parent trust
      ↓
More bookings
      ↓
More coach earnings
      ↓
More great coaches
      ↓
More community activity
      ↓
More marketplace liquidity
      ↓
More daily usage
```

If a campaign, channel, or partnership does not move at least one arrow, do not run it.

## The three growth engines (priority order)

### 1. Coach acquisition

Acquire **elite** coaches.

- **Curated** — approval required; quality over volume.
- **Relationship-driven** — not performance marketing at scale (yet).
- **Dense geographically** — clusters beat national sprawl.

**Near-term target:** **30 earning coaches** before broad parent demand — not 30 approvals. One earning coach is worth ten inactive ones.

Primary surfaces: `/coaches`, `/signup/coach`, homepage coach CTA, direct outreach.

---

### 2. Coach success

**The most important growth function.**

A coach that earns **$500/month** recruits another coach.  
A coach that earns **$0** leaves.

**Success > acquisition.**

Coach success includes: calendar published, bookings, payouts, repeat athletes, reviews, and eventually weekly Guild habit (see `docs/VISION.md`).

Growth and operations own the coach experience after approval — not “someone else’s problem.”

---

### 3. Parent demand

Demand should **follow** coach density.

**Never create demand without supply.**

Parent marketing turns on only when a geography meets expansion criteria (below). Until then, parent growth is organic and local — word of mouth, coach networks, reviews, and coach-led announcements.

---

## Geographic expansion rules

**Never launch a geography. Win a geography.**

Do not run parent acquisition, paid ads, or “we’re live in [city]” campaigns until **all** minimum criteria are met in that market:

| Criterion | Minimum |
|-----------|---------|
| Active coaches | **10** |
| Completed sessions | **100** |
| Five-star reviews | **25** |
| Recurring families | **50** |

**Only then** expand — next adjacent market, same playbook.

Wrestling beachhead first (NC and adjacent states where we have credibility and coach relationships). Multi-region sprawl before density is an anti-goal.

---

## Recruiting philosophy

| Era | Mode | Meaning |
|-----|------|---------|
| **Today** | **Relationships** | You recruit — texts, team rooms, tournaments, alumni, club directors |
| **Tomorrow** | **Brand** | `/coaches`, live stats, practice photography, and proof pull inbound interest |
| **Eventually** | **Referrals** | Coaches recruit coaches; Guild recruits itself |

Early Guild wins through **trust and touch**, not ad spend. Invest in relationships until brand and earnings stories do the work.

## Every coach is marketing

You are not recruiting coaches. You are recruiting **micro-influencers**.

Each approved college or club coach brings an audience — often **500–2,000** followers on Instagram. When they post *“I’m now coaching through The Guild”* with a booking link, that is growth — not a side effect.

**Every approved coach should receive a launch kit:**

- Welcome graphic (personalized)
- Instagram post (static)
- Reel template or clip guidance
- Profile link (`/coaches/[id]`)
- Booking link
- Schedule announcement copy
- Story assets (stickers, frames, swipe-up / link-in-bio guidance)

Document the kit. Ship it consistently. **Write down what you already do** so it scales beyond founder-led onboarding.

Founding Coach program (through **September 1, 2026**): featured placement, locked fee, badge, exclusive apparel — see `/coaches`.

## Activation

**Acquisition ends at approval. Growth begins at first payout.**

Marketing and growth **own activation** — not Product alone. Product enables; Growth drives the coach through the funnel.

### Activation definition

```
Approved
      ↓
Calendar published
      ↓
First booking
      ↓
First payout
      ↓
Second booking
      ↓
Recurring athlete
```

Track drop-off at every step. Founding coaches get **white-glove onboarding** (e.g. 15-minute call: rate card, availability, first slot live before hang up).

---

## Product-led growth (when to market what)

**Product expansion marketing comes after coaches open Guild weekly.**

Do not lead campaigns with AI, community, or marketplace features until the Intelligent Coach Platform supports weekly coach habit (`docs/VISION.md` — Current state). Market what retains and earns — booking, payouts, verified profiles, coach success stories — first.

| Ship first | Market when true |
|------------|------------------|
| Unified coach home | “One app before every lesson” |
| Coach OS intelligence (rebook, open slots) | Quiet time-saved stories — not chatbot hype |
| Recurring sessions | “Set it once; get paid every week” |
| New geography | Only after expansion criteria met |

## Founding Coach sprint (now → September 1, 2026)

**Goal:** **30 earning coaches** — not 30 applications.

**Message:** *You coach. We handle scheduling, payments, and parent trust — you keep about 80–90%.*  
Not “join a marketplace.” Not “AI-powered platform.”

**Channels (wrestling, relationship-first):**

- Direct outreach to NCAA / NAIA / JUCO wrestlers and club coaches
- Team rooms, clubs, tournaments, camps — QR to `/coaches`, Guild apparel
- Coach referrals (tracked links; mutual benefit)
- Instagram — real practice photography only
- Alumni and teammate networks

**Funnel metrics:**

```
/coaches visits → Apply → Approved → Calendar published → First booking → First payout → Second booking → Recurring athlete
```

## Growth metrics (report these)

| Metric | Owner |
|--------|--------|
| **Earning coaches** (≥1 payout in 30 days) | Growth |
| **Activated coaches** (calendar published within 7 days of approval) | Growth |
| Applications / week | Growth |
| Approval rate and time-to-approve | Operations |
| Bookings per earning coach | Growth + Product |
| **Weekly active coaches** | Product + Growth |
| Parent bookings in **won** geographies only | Growth |
| Referrals (coach → coach) | Growth |

Do not optimize for page views, raw signups, or follower counts without activation context.

---

## Do this, not that

| Situation | **Do** | **Don't** |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Measuring growth | **Earning coaches**, payouts, repeat bookings | Applications, traffic, signups alone |
| Parent campaigns | Turn on when **supply exists** in that geo | National ads with thin coach supply |
| Recruiting | **Relationships** and launch kits per coach | Open enrollment or “anyone can coach” |
| New market | **Win** one geography first | “Launch” in ten cities at once |
| Feature marketing | **After** weekly coach habit | Lead with AI / community before OS retention |
| Coach quality | Curated, verified, premium | Volume for vanity metrics |

## Anti-goals (growth)

- Not growth hacking without activation ownership.
- Not parent demand without coach density.
- Not geographic sprawl before winning a market.
- Not treating coaches as listings instead of **micro-influencers**.
- Not competing on ad spend with well-funded marketplaces before earnings stories exist.
- Not multi-sport recruiting before wrestling density and coach success are proven.

Align with product anti-goals in `docs/VISION.md`.
